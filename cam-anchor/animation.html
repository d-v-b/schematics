Cam anchor in motion

Four log-spiral cams on an M8 axle, looking along the beams into the 80 mm gap. The rubber bands set the cams against the walls, and a hanging load turns them harder in. The outlines are the printed parts, and the forces come from the anchor's mechanics model.

trigger cord rubber band wall reaction

Cycle

  1. At rest: the bands hold the cams fully open
  2. Pull the trigger to fold the cams in
  3. Push the anchor up into the gap
  4. Let go: the bands swing the cams to the walls
  5. Hang the load: the cams bite and turn further out
  6. Unload, pull the trigger, take it out
